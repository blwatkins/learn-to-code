## Introduction

Cryptocurrency wallets are software applications that provide a secure way to manage digital assets and interact with blockchain networks.
Cryptocurrency wallets do not store the cryptocurrency itself, but rather the private keys that allow users to access and manage their digital assets on the blockchain.
